Projects

Posture-based stress estimation

Research paper, in progressJan 2026 – ongoing

An explainable pipeline built on 13,143 cleaned MPII Human Pose samples. ViTPose extracts 17 body keypoints from raw images, five geometric posture features feed an evidence-weighted composite stress score, and an XGBoost regressor reaches R² 0.52 and AUC 0.97 on a held-out test set.

Five-fold cross-validation gives a mean R² of 0.54 ± 0.02. A single-feature ablation shows the five-feature fusion beats every single-angle model, and SHAP identifies head tilt as the strongest predictor.

Python, XGBoost, SHAP, ViTPose, Hugging Face

Vehicle counting and tracking from traffic video

Aug – Sep 2026

An image processing pipeline for 1080p traffic video (75 sampled frames): median-background segmentation, Otsu thresholding, morphological filtering and a distance-transform watershed to split merged vehicles.

Geometric features (area, centroid, perimeter, eccentricity, solidity) feed a minimum-distance classifier that sorts 1,580 detections into small, medium and large, with no labelled training data. Tracking produced 247 vehicle tracks, 78.5% of them lasting beyond one frame. Counting accuracy reached 85.4% (MAE 3.5 vehicles per frame) against manual counts on 4 sampled frames.

Python, OpenCV, scikit-image, NumPy, Pandas, SciPy, Matplotlib
